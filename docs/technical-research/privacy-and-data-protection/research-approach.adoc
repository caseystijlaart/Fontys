That means literature and document study of data-minimisation practice and AVG/GDPR legislation.
The Workshop strategy those rows also list, an expert consultation, is not part of this chapter.
This chapter is a desk-research analysis based on existing literature only.

== Library: Literature and Document Study

The search covers two distinct bodies of source material. They are kept separate because they answer different questions.
The first is the legal text itself and regulator guidance interpreting it: the GDPR, its Dutch implementing act (the UAVG), and Article 29 Working Party/EDPB opinions on anonymisation and on when a Data Protection Impact Assessment is required.
The second is the technical literature already gathered in Non-Camera Person Position & Height Sensing Techniques on how identifying a given sensor's output actually is.
The most direct source is Su et al.'s privacy-sensitive mmWave pose-estimation dataset (<<su2024mmfreepose,2024>>).
It is used here to check the legal anonymisation test against this project's specific sensor choice, not mmWave point clouds in the abstract.
Document analysis cross-references this chapter's conclusions against the data-minimisation decisions Candidate Sensor Technologies and Module Selection already made. The sensor choice is not treated as a given input to accept uncritically.
