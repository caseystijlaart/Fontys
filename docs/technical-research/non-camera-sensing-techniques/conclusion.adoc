= Conclusion

Candidate Sensor Technologies and Module Selection recommend mmWave radar, specifically the IWR6843AOPEVM: the only approach with keypoint-level pose literature for height estimation, non-identifying output, and the strongest literature base of any candidate reviewed, whose antenna-on-package design also gives the elevation coverage needed for room-scale height at the lowest price of the variants compared.

Implementation risk is managed by building the mmWave pipeline in capability-based phases (position, then height, then multi-person clustering, then speaker detection), each on an already-working previous phase; mmWave remains the most expensive candidate reviewed, so mentor approval of its cost is a precondition, not a detail to resolve later.
Sensor fusion (mmWave or 2D LiDAR with a thermal sensor) was deliberately not adopted as the starting recommendation: mmWave alone already has a plausible path to a height estimate, none of the fusion combinations has been tested together in the literature, and the thermal side needs two units to match mmWave's coverage, adding real cost rather than a marginal one.
Fusion remains a documented fallback if the single-sensor result underperforms, not a precondition for starting.
