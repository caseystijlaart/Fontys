= Mutex


== Synchronization 


=== Step 1

In the first step, the sprinklers are synchronized in such a way that orders for a specific sprinkler will have to wait until the sprinkler is already in use.
To achieve this, each sprinkler acquires a mutex when the sprinkler is turned on.
Once the sprinkler is turned off again, the mutex is released.


=== Step 3

A mutex is added to each scene to prevent it from being executed more than once at the same time. 
The mutex is acquired when the scene starts and released when it is over.
