= AI/ML-Based Plant Health Monitoring Systems

In contrast to traditional IoT-based plant monitoring systems, recent developments have begun integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques.
These systems are capable of not only monitoring environmental parameters but also analyzing data to make intelligent predictions, detect anomalies, and optimize plant care.

AI/ML-based plant health monitoring systems can:  

* Predict irrigation needs based on soil moisture trends and weather conditions.
* Detect early signs of plant stress or disease using sensor data or images.
* Provide automated recommendations or interventions to improve plant health.
* Learn and adapt to the specific requirements of different plant species over time.

These advancements enable more proactive and efficient plant management, surpassing the reactive threshold-based alerts of traditional systems.

**Examples of AI/ML-based systems include:**  

* IoT-Based Smart Plant Monitoring System (Emergent Mind) [[[ai_plant_emergentmind]]]
* AI-Enabled IoT-Based Smart Indoor Plant Care System (Academia.edu) [[[ai_plant_academia]]]
* Deep Learning for Plant Health Monitoring (Nature Scientific Reports) [[[ai_plant_nature]]]
* AI-Driven Smart Plant Monitoring (IJSAT Journal) [[[ai_plant_ijsat]]]
* Plant Health Monitoring System (GitHub) [[[ai_plant_github]]]

These systems illustrate the potential for combining IoT hardware with AI/ML algorithms to achieve intelligent, autonomous plant care and monitoring.

These existing approaches highlight the potential for enhancement by integrating AI and ML techniques, which could enable predictive care, anomaly detection, and intelligent decision-making for plant health and irrigation management.
