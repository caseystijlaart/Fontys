= Timeline

There are multiple timelines that will guide the course of the project.
The first timeline is the overall timeline, which gives the basic structure of the project, when the sprint are, etc.
The second timeline is focussed on the tasks that are preformed during each sprint.

== Overall Timeline

The timeline given in <<Project Timeline>> shows the overall overview timeline of the project, and when some important events take place.

## Tasks Timeline

The phases, and the associated tasks, are given in a table to give an clear overview of how the project is approach and estimation of phasing realization.
The table <<Expected Tasks and phasing duration>> is more detailed and will give 'more' detail on the expected tasks that will be preformed during the sprint.
In the timeline <<Phasing Timeline>> a more sketched out overview of the phasing throughout the weeks and sprints are given.

[.table]

|===
| Sprint | Phase | Tasks

| 1 | Analysis and Problem Definition | Research the problem, the solutions, and its requirements. Next to this, create the concrete plan.
| 2 | Design | Create the design.
| 3 | Realization | Implementation and testing.
| 4 | Realization | Implementation and testing.
| 5 | Realization / Evaluation | Implementation, testing and final realization.
